# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, or experience level.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good faith and welcome newcomers.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.
- Accept feedback gracefully and give it kindly.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Personal or political attacks.
- Publishing others' private information without consent.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when representing the project in public.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it privately to **borelineapp@proton.me**. Reports are handled confidentially, and maintainers will respond as promptly as they can.

This Code of Conduct is adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
